Made for Windows

Personal, practical, and local.

ChronoDesk is designed to stay lightweight while giving you control over what appears on your desktop.

Language & layout

Switch between English and Persian from the system tray. The Solar Hijri calendar uses Persian weekday names and B Roya typography when the font is installed, including in the English interface.

  • Independent widget visibility
  • Always-on-top option
  • Optional launch at Windows sign-in

Local data by default

Tasks, notes, preferences, window position, and network history are stored in your local application data folder. Market quotes are retrieved from public TGJU pages.

  • No account required
  • No third-party runtime packages
  • Network history is collected while the app is running
